### [probe] Why is the gap 0, and why are incomplete folds allowed?

- Skills: choosing-a-forecaster, forecasting-single-series, backtesting-configuration; 14.2s

A gap of 0 indicates that forecasts begin immediately after the training window with no delay between data availability and prediction time. Allowing incomplete folds ensures that the final evaluation fold is retained even if the remaining observations in the dataset do not fill the full 36-step forecast horizon.

## Gap Parameter

* `gap=0` represents a deployment scenario where predictions are made in real time, with zero delay between the end of observed data and the first forecast step.
* Setting a non-zero gap is only needed when there is an ingestion delay or operational lag between data collection and forecast generation, which is not present in this setup.

## Incomplete Fold Handling

* `allow_incomplete_fold=True` allows the final fold in the backtesting sequence to run even when fewer observations than the configured 36 steps remain.
* This setting avoids discarding remaining observations at the end of the series, ensuring the evaluation evaluates across the full date range up to 2012-12-31 23:00:00 across the 17 folds.
