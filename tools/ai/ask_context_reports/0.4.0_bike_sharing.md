### [grounded] For hourly demand with strong daily and weekly seasonality, when should I prefer a direct forecasting strategy over a recursive one?

- Skills: choosing-a-forecaster; 6.6s

You should prefer a direct forecasting strategy over a recursive one when the predictive relationship between historical demand and future demand changes significantly across the forecast horizon. It is also beneficial when error propagation over longer horizons degrades recursive forecasts, because direct forecasting trains a dedicated model for each forecast step. However, this approach requires training multiple models and fixing the horizon at initialization.

## When to Prefer Direct Forecasting

* Horizon-dependent relationships: Predicting demand 1 hour ahead often depends on recent momentum and immediate lags, whereas predicting 24 hours or 168 hours ahead relies much more on daily or weekly seasonal cycles. A direct strategy (`ForecasterDirect`) trains one independent model per step, allowing each step to learn step-specific patterns.
* Mitigating error propagation: In recursive forecasting (`ForecasterRecursive`), predictions are fed back as inputs for subsequent steps, which can cause estimation errors to compound over long horizons. Direct models use only observed historical data as inputs for every step, completely avoiding error accumulation.
* Fixed forecast horizons: When your operational workflow always targets a specific, fixed number of future hours (such as a 24-hour day-ahead schedule), the fixed-horizon requirement of direct forecasting fits naturally.

## Trade-offs to Consider

* Training time and memory: Direct forecasting fits N separate estimators for an N-step horizon, which increases training time and memory usage compared to the single estimator used in recursive forecasting. You can mitigate training time using the `n_jobs` parameter to fit models in parallel.
* Fixed horizon at initialization: You must define `steps` when initializing `ForecasterDirect`, meaning you cannot predict beyond that preconfigured horizon without retraining. `ForecasterRecursive` remains flexible to predict any number of steps at prediction time.
* Starting recommendation: `ForecasterRecursive` is generally recommended as the starting default for single series due to its simplicity, speed, and flexibility. Direct forecasting is best reserved for scenarios where recursive error accumulation is noticeable or when step-specific dynamics justify the extra computational cost.
