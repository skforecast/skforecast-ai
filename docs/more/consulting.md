# Consulting and professional services

The core team of skforecast and skforecast-ai works with companies that forecast with machine learning, from a first model to one running in production:

- **Forecasting projects**: demand, sales, energy or any other time series, modeled and validated the way they will be used.
- **Auditable modeling with LLMs**: the approach behind skforecast-ai, where every decision comes from a rule and the LLM explains it, applied to your own workflows.
- **Machine learning**: classification, regression and anomaly detection.
- **Advisory**: choosing the right approach for your data, your team and your goals.
- **Workshops and training**: hands-on courses on forecasting with skforecast, adapted to your team.
