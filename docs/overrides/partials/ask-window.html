    "recursive one?"

For hourly demand with strong daily and weekly seasonality, you should prefer a direct forecasting strategy when you need to avoid error propagation over long horizons or when the predictive relationship changes significantly across the forecast horizon. Because a direct forecaster trains an independent model for each step, it is highly effective at capturing step-specific patterns without letting early-step prediction errors compound over a 24-hour or 168-hour cycle.
